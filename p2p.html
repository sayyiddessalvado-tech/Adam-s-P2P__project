Bank-Grade Security

How We Protect Lenders & Borrowers

Our multi-layered security architecture ensures that your funds and data remain completely shielded from risk.

🔑

Verified Academic Identity

No anonymous profiles. Every student must authenticate their identity using verified school administration portals before requesting a loan.

🔒

Escrow Smart Holding

Lender funds are never sent directly to a borrower's personal account. They are secured in escrow until the required educational collateral is locked.

📊

Double-Entry Wallet

Every transaction uses strict database ledger protocols. Money can never "disappear" or be modified arbitrarily in the database.

⚡

Instant Auto-Recovery

Our autonomous sweep system instantly routes incoming student wallet deposits to clear active past-due loan balances.
